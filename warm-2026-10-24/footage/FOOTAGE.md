# Background footage: Adobe Stock (free collection)

The video replaces the poster. No event footage exists, so the background is real (non-AI) footage
from Adobe Stock, **free collection**, licensed on 28.09.2026 through the connected Adobe account.
The .mov files are **not** stored in the repo (4K, ~100 MB each, and stock licences don't allow redistribution);
they can be re-downloaded from the Adobe Stock account using the IDs below.

Free sites (Pexels / Pixabay / Mixkit) are blocked from the work environment.

## Grade
All clips go through the same grade (reference magenta): saturation ×0.55, colour balance pushed
to red/blue with green pulled back, blacks lifted to ~4 %, light haze. Before/after: `selection_raw_vs_graded.jpg`.

## Caveat
The DJ in #3 is **not** one of the lineup artists. He is shown only from behind, and names are never
placed over his face, so nobody can read him as JUST2 / Salvi Fernandez / CORAL / BITCH. If you want
zero ambiguity, #3 can be swapped for #6 (hands only).
